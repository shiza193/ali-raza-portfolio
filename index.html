About Me

Turning Ideas Into
Digital Reality

I'm a React Native Product Engineer with 4+ years of experience building production-grade mobile applications for startups and businesses — focused on stable architecture, real scalability, and performance that holds up after launch, not just during development.

I help teams solve the problems that surface after real users arrive: apps that break or slow down at scale, MVPs that can't evolve into full products, weak architecture that turns into a maintenance burden, and complex native or hardware integrations. Where it genuinely adds value, I also layer in AI-powered features — automation, smart flows, intelligent UX — as a functional part of the app, never a gimmick.
